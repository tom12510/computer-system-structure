  • Parts of Speech

  • a/an：可数名词单数，用于首次提到的名词

eg：I saw a cat in the garden，She is an engineer，in a hurry, have a cold

  • 零冠词：用于泛指、抽象概念或固定表达（不可数名词或复数名词）

  • the：特指已知或唯一的事物（序数词，最高级，组织群体，乐器名词）

  • 量化词：可数名词（many, a few,each, every, a number of），不可数名词（much, a little, a piece of,a large amount of,a great deal of），通用（some，any，a lot of/lots of，most，all，enough）

  • 名词复数

  • 以 s, ss, x, sh, ch 结尾的词：在单词末尾加 -es

  • 以辅音字母 + y 结尾的词：将 y 改为 i，再加 -es

  • 不规则变化：man → men, woman → women, foot → feet, tooth → teeth，sheep → sheep, deer → deer, fish → fish

  • 抽象名词：the+形容词，动名词

  • 人称代词

  • 形容性物主代词：与名词连用，限定名词所属关系，不与冠词使用（My，your，his/her/its/our/their)

  • 名词性物主代词：独立表达所属关系（mine，yours，his/hers/its/ours/theirs）

eg：This book is mine. That car is hers.

  • 反身代词：指代句子的主语，谓语动词执行者与承受者相同

eg：She cut herself while cooking，He bought a gift for himself

  • 相互代词：表示两者或更多者之间相互的行为为或关系（each other / one other)

过去式/过去分词：

  1. 存在第一个助动词（be/have/will）决定过去/现在/将来时态

  2. 存在第二个助动词（be）决定被动/进行/完成时态

  3. 过去时态：过去某个发生的动作（yesterday，last night，two day ago，at 3 PM）

  4. 完成时态（have/has + 过去分词）：过去发生的动作对现在的影响（already，ever，since，for）

  5. 被动语态 （am/is/are + 过去分词）：强调动作的承受者

  6. 完成时被动语态（have/has + been + 过去分词）：强调该动作的结果或状态

  7. 虚拟语句：使用过去时段表达假设

    eg：If she had known about the meeting, she would have attended it

    ​ She acts as if she were a queen.

    ​ He talks as though he knew everything

    ​ She behaves as though she might become famous overnight

  • Sentence Structure
  • 补语：描述主语/宾语状态或特征，与系动词搭配使用
  • 表语：位于系动词之后，说明主语身份、性质、特征或状态
  • 定语：修饰名词或代词（形容词，名词，数词）
  • 状语：修饰动词、形容词、副词或整个句子的成分，用于说明动作或状态的时间、地点、原因、方式、程度
  • 同位语：紧跟在一个名词或代词之后，进一步解释、定义或补充前面的名词或代词

非谓语：可作除谓语外其它句子成分，无人称和数的变化，

  • 不定式（主语/宾语/定语/状语）
  • to + 动词原形：表目的/计划/将来/抽象概念（To learn English takes time and effort）
  • to be done：当强调动作的承受者而非执行者（The car needs to be washed）
  • to be doing：强调正在进行的动作，主句的动作同时发生（She pretended to be listening, but she was actually thinking about her vacation.）
  • to have done：当强调动作已完成且关注的是动作的承受者时（It is expected that the report will have been finished by Friday）
  • 动名词（名词性质，作主语/宾语/表语.介词）
  • doing：I enjoy listening to music
  • being done：表示被动和正在进行的状态（He was excited about being chosen for the project team.）
  • having done：强调某个动作在另一个动作之前已经完成时（Having finished his homework, Tom went out to play with his friends.）
  • 分词（doing/done，形容词）：表示谓语动作的状态或进展

倒装句：助动词，不及物动词或副词提前，突出句首的特定信息

  1. 一般疑问句：助动词/系动词提前 Do you like coffee?

  2. 特殊疑问句：疑问词 + 一般疑问句语序 What are you doing?

  3. 否定倒装（用于强调否定意义或表达惊讶、怀疑等情感）：否定词 + 助动词 + 主语 + 实义动词

  4. Never have I seen such a beautiful sunset（I have never seen such a beautiful sunset）

  5. Not until midnight did he finish his work.（He did not finish his work until midnight）

  6. Rarely do we go to the cinema these days.（We rarely go to the cinema these days.）

  7. 条件倒装（强调某一情况或条件）：Only + 状语（副词/介词短语/从句） + 助动词 + 主语

  8. Only when he arrived home did he realize his mistake（He realized his mistake only when he arrived home）

  9. Only by working hard can one succeed（One can succeed only by working hard.）

  10. 结构/程度倒装：So/(Such) + 形容词/副词（名词） + 助动词 + 主语 + that

  11. So boring was the movie that I fell asleep halfway through it. (The movie was so boring that I fell asleep halfway through it)

  12. Such a noise was there that no one could sleep （There was such a noise that no one could sleep ）

  13. 虚拟条件：Were/Had/Should + 主语 + 其他

  14. 让步状语倒装：形容词/名词/副词/动词原形 + as/though + 主语 + 谓语

  15. Try as she might, she couldn't open the door

  16. Young as he is, he speaks five languages.（Even though he is young, he speaks five languages.）

主从句：从句充当主句的成分（主/宾/表/同位/定/状语）

  1. 主语从句（that/whether/wh-词）

  2. That the Earth revolves around the sun is a scientific fact

  3. Whether she will accept the offer remains to be seen.
  4. It is important that we start early. （we start early is important)
  5. It seems that he has changed his mind.（he seems to have changed his mind）
  6. It annoyed everyone that she was late.（That she was late annoyed everyone）
  7. Who will win the competition is anyone's guess

  8. How much it will cost depends on the materials.

  9. 宾语从句（that/whether/if/wh-词）

  10. I wonder whether/if he will attend the meeting.

  11. Can you tell me whose book this is?

  13. 表语从句：主语 + 系动词 + 引导词 + 从句

  14. The problem is that we don't have enough resources.

  15. The question is whether we can finish the project on time.

  16. The issue might be where the funds were allocated

  17. 同位语从句（名词性从句）：抽象名词 + 引导词 + 完整句子

  • The news that we won the game spread quickly.（We won the game. This news spread quickly）

  • There is some doubt whether he will come on time （He may not come on time. This creates some doubt.）

  • 定语从句（修饰名词或代词）：先行词 + 关系词 + 从句

  • The person whom I met yesterday is very friendly.
  • The book that I read last week was fascinating.
  • This is the town where I grew up.

  • My sister, who lives in London, is coming to visit next month.

  • 状语从句（修饰主句的动词，形容词，表达句子时间、条件、原因等逻辑关系）

  • I was reading while she was cooking.

  • He got up early so that he could catch the first bus.

一般过去时 did was/were + done yesterday, last week They built this bridge in 1990. → This bridge was built in 1990.

过去完成时 had + done had been + done by 2010, before left We had packed the boxes. → The boxes had been packed.
一般将来时 will do will be + done tomorrow, next month They will announce the results. → The results will be announced.
将来进行时 will be doing 无被动形式 at this time tomorrow
将来完成时 will have + done will have been + done by 2030, in two years She will have completed the project. → The project will have been completed.
